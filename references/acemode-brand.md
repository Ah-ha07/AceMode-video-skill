# AceMode brand profile

Use this profile only when the user asks for an AceMode-branded video. It governs identity, logo treatment, color, framing, and the brand close. It does not rewrite narration or add promotional claims unless the user asks for copy changes.

## Working brand definition

AceMode is an **AI-native productivity community for builders, operators, founders, creators, and ambitious knowledge workers**. In Chinese, use the compact category label `AI 实践者效率社区`.

The community turns fast-moving AI signals into tested tools, concrete workflows, and time leverage. Its public promise is:

`10X faster, log off earlier`

Treat AceMode as three things working together:

1. **Signal desk** — identify consequential AI launches, agents, models, and workflow shifts early.
2. **Field lab** — test what a tool can actually do instead of repeating launch claims.
3. **Playbook community** — translate findings into practical choices that help members work faster and reclaim time.

Do not position AceMode as a generic AI news feed, hustle-culture club, guru brand, or software vendor unless the user explicitly changes the company positioning. The editorial attitude is sharp, credible, practical, optimistic, and anti-hype.

## Logo system

The master mark is the `AceMode` wordmark with exactly this capitalization. The LinkedIn reference shows a near-white wordmark on a solid deep navy field. Until an official vector master is supplied, treat the wordmark—not an invented symbol—as the source of identity.

- Primary lockup: `AceMode` in near-white on Ace Navy.
- Avatar lockup: centered wordmark inside a square Ace Navy tile.
- Light-background lockup: Ace Navy wordmark without a tile, only when contrast is sufficient.
- Preserve a clear space around the wordmark of at least the cap height of the `A`.
- Keep the tagline outside the logo clear space; never fuse it into the wordmark.
- Never alter the capitalization, stretch the letters, add bevels, or apply gradients to the wordmark.
- Do not use the colorful gradient `A` seen in an earlier sample outro as an official AceMode logo. It is an unapproved campaign graphic.
- Do not invent a monogram, ace-of-spades symbol, play button, robot, or community badge. Request the official asset if a symbol is required.

If the official logo asset is unavailable, render a temporary text lockup with a heavy neutral grotesk and mark it for brand review. Do not trace a low-resolution social screenshot into a supposed master logo.

## Core visual tokens

The navy and near-white values below are sampled from the supplied LinkedIn avatar. The cyan is the established signal accent in the existing AceMode video sample.

| Role | Token | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ace Navy | `#162D51` | Primary logo tile, identity panels, brand close |
| Midnight | `#0D1A2B` | Deep editorial backgrounds and banners |
| Cloud | `#F0F6FA` | Wordmark and high-contrast type |
| Signal Cyan | `#2EE6D6` | Verified signal, active line, selected emphasis |
| Ink | `#090B12` | Near-black editorial field |

Use cyan as a signal, not as wallpaper. Allow topic-specific secondary accents for charts and evidence, but keep them out of the master wordmark. Prefer restrained atmospheric light trails, thin data lines, subtle grid structure, and editorial texture over glossy SaaS cards or loud cyberpunk neon.

## Typography and voice in the frame

Use a heavy modern grotesk for display headlines and the wordmark fallback, a clean sans serif for body copy, and a compact mono face for evidence labels and timestamps. High contrast and compressed hierarchy matter more than novelty.

Brand language should feel like an informed peer who has tested the tool:

- lead with the signal or result;
- distinguish verified fact from inference;
- explain why it changes the viewer's work;
- end with a practical implication, not empty motivation.

`Stay sharp. Stay ahead.` may be used as an editorial sign-off. It is a campaign line, not a replacement for the LinkedIn promise `10X faster, log off earlier`.

## Video placement

- Opening: use a small `ACE MODE / AI SIGNALS` or `AceMode` publisher bug; never open with a disconnected logo animation before the evidence hook.
- Body: keep branding quiet. A small publisher bug or one thin Signal Cyan rule is enough.
- Brand close: resolve to the AceMode wordmark on Ace Navy or Midnight. The spoken story does not have to say AceMode unless requested.
- Community descriptor: use `AI 实践者效率社区` in Chinese or `AI-native productivity community` in English when context needs to explain what AceMode is.
- Do not place the logo over faces, source labels, captions, or official third-party logos.

## Brand review gate

Before presenting an AceMode preview, verify all of the following:

- `AceMode` capitalization is exact.
- The wordmark uses Ace Navy/Cloud or another approved one-color contrast treatment.
- No unapproved `A` monogram or gradient logo appears.
- The brand is described as an AI-native productivity/practitioner community, not a generic news account.
- `10X faster, log off earlier` is treated as the public promise.
- Branding remains subordinate to the evidence and does not turn the documentary into an advertisement.
